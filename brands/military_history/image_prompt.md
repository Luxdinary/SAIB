# Frontlore - Image prompt template

For AI-generated stills (Phase 2+) and for the placeholder generator (Phase 1). Every generated image is logged in the manifest with model, prompt and licence.

## Base prompt (English, prepend to every scene prompt)

```
documentary tech, olive green, paper grain, contour map lines, archive labels with dates in monospace, stylized diagrams, never photorealistic battle scenes. Vertical 9:16 composition, subject centered in the upper two thirds, lower third kept calm for captions. Colours: background #29352D, accent #A9B29F, text #E6E9E5. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no faces of real people.
```

## Negative prompt

```
text, watermark, logo, real person, celebrity, uniform, blood, weapon handling, low quality, blurry, stock photo look
```

## Scene prompt pattern

`<what is shown>, <camera or diagram type>, <mood word from tone>` - one subject per image, no collages.

## Rules

- No realistic depictions of real people, places or events (EU AI Act Art. 50 disclosure would otherwise be required). Prefer stylized, diagrammatic or abstract imagery.
- no tactics, no weapon handling or construction, no current conflicts, nothing from Stefan's service, no uniform, no graphic violence; realistic AI scenes of real events need the Art. 50 disclosure at the start, so prefer stylized visuals.
